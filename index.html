I am a mathematics undergraduate in the Chern Class at Nankai University, where I expect to graduate in June 2027. I conduct research in Prof. Ming-Ming Cheng’s group at the College of Computer Science, advised by Prof. Qing Guo. I study AI security, with a focus on the security and evaluation of LLM agents.

I graduated from Zhonghua High School in Nanjing in 2023, with a perfect physics score in that year's Jiangsu Gaokao. Outside of academics, I enjoy running, badminton, and hiking.

Within my class, I have served as class monitor, League branch secretary, academic representative, and wellness officer.
